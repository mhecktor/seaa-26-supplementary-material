visitor class This is at the cost of increased indirection due to the double dispatch required when the algorithm is invoked We experimentally investigate the energy impact of varying the implementation of this pattern, and of removing the pattern entirely from software written in Java and C++ In our results we observe energy consumption reductions greater than 7% in a Java-based textbook example when the pattern is implemented using reflective dispatch, and reductions of over 10% when experimenting with an open source Java project, JavaParser The complete removal of the pattern yields more complex results, with little impact in the textbook example but reductions of over 7% in the JavaParser study To explore the generalisability of our findings, we subsequently apply
